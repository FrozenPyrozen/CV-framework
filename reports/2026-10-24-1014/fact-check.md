# Fact check review

## Verdict
The changed pages are well sourced. Nothing is invented, every number matches its source, and every *Technologies* list matches section 5 or the project's stack. The HERE navigation app page follows the published portfolio and `navigation-statistics` 1 and 4.1 to 4.5 closely. On the car navigation PoC page, three sentences use stronger verbs than the git summary: "added" where it says "migrated", "adapted" or "refined", and "built" where it says "adjusted". A few smaller wording and caption points remain. One finding from the last review (OMO "tech lead ... for a sprint") is still open.
