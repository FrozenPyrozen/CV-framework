# Confidentiality review (portfolio)

## Verdict
The built portfolio (A4 and Letter Word and PDF, checked 2026-10-23 16:33, `npm run status`: ok) contains no Section 9 term, no internal URL, no colleague names and no phone number. The only links are the profile's own GitHub and LinkedIn. The text and the Word files' alt text never name the two hidden clients, and the Jira plugin screenshots have the plugin name covered. The remaining risks come from text kept from the published 2025-09 portfolio and from the screenshots. The PoC page names the end customer (Volkswagen AG, Porsche) and in-car feature names under an "under NDA" title. The clinical-trial page repeats the hidden client's own product wording almost word for word. Several screenshots show what may be real people's data (a Snapchat handle and photos, including a profile marked 17 years old, a user's photo, smart-home device IDs, a wallet address). Every image is marked `public: yes, confirmed by user`.
