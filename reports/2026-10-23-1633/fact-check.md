# Fact check review

## Verdict
The portfolio is well sourced. Every project cites only itself, every number matches section 7 or a confirmed fact, and the text kept from the published portfolio (`doc:portfolio-2025-09`) is close to the original. No claim is invented. The problems are in places where the old portfolio text says more than the summaries, or something different: chunked uploads and on-time delivery on the Jira page, "core flow" on the clinical trial page, "tech lead for a sprint" at OMO, and *Technologies* lists that include items the summaries mark as "used by the project" or don't list at all.

- **Claims checked:** 129 source-tagged sentences and lists (16 project pages; the cover, titles and screenshot blocks are generated and were checked only for captions).
- **Supported:** 110
- **Partly supported:** 19 (6 sentences, 13 *Technologies* lists)
- **Unsupported:** 0
- **Numbers:** all match. "more than 1,800 ERP menu items" (section 7: 1,891, decision `keep-erp-programs-figure`), "days to hours" (`syspro-speed`), "8 developers (6 React Native, 1 iOS, 1 Android)" (GM summary 4.1), "3x faster" build (`gm-poc-build-3x`), "2 minutes to 5 seconds" (Velas section 7, user-confirmed), "10 screens" (`dosimeter-10-screens`), "about 17 component specifications" (SYSPRO section 7, "~17"), "more than 5 million registered users" (Itjet 4.8, confirmed 2026-10-23). No `doc:` number appears without a confirmation.
- **Captions:** checked against the images for SYSPRO (program menu, purchase order), the Jira plugin (both forms) and Wink (all four, including the three new captions). They describe what the screenshots show.
